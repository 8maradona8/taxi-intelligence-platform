# Taxi Intelligence Platform — Project Charter

## Mission

Taxi Intelligence Platform helps professional drivers make better location decisions by collecting urban data, transforming it into unified signals, and generating real-time demand recommendations.

## Product Vision

TIP is not just a dashboard. It is an intelligence system that answers:

> Where should a driver be in the next 15–30 minutes to increase the probability of higher income?

## Target User

The first target user is a professional taxi driver operating in Sofia, Bulgaria.

Future expansion may include:
- other Bulgarian cities
- courier services
- delivery drivers
- logistics operators
- urban mobility analytics

## Core Intelligence Modules

- Airport Intelligence
- Transport Intelligence
- Event Intelligence
- Traffic Intelligence
- Weather Intelligence
- Demand Engine
- Driver Dashboard

## North Star

Transform fragmented city information into clear, actionable driver recommendations.

## Engineering Principles

- Modular monolith first
- Signals as the unified domain language
- Clean separation between collectors, services, repositories, and database
- Every sprint must produce a demonstrable result
- Documentation is part of the product
